(the two clear buttons)		Switches between VoiceOver mode and TD Snap mode. The box gives no sound or light when it switches.
Back + GreenTurns VoiceOver's Quick Nav on or off. Rarely needed.

Which mode is it in?

  • With VoiceOver on, turn the wheel.
  • VoiceOver moves from item to item = VoiceOver mode.
  • Nothing happens = TD Snap mode.
  • The box remembers its mode when unplugged.
